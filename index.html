Croissant

A croissant is a classic French pastry known for its crescent shape, buttery flavor, and flaky texture. Made from laminated dough, a process that involves folding butter into the dough multiple times, croissants develop delicate layers that puff up beautifully when baked. They are typically enjoyed fresh in the morning, either plain or filled with ingredients like chocolate, almond paste and cheese. The croissant has become a beloved symbol of French baking and is popular worldwide for its rich taste and elegant appearance.
